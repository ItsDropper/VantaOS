# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The VantaOS community aims to be a respectful, constructive place to build and discuss an operating system. We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, viewpoints, and abilities. Participation in this project is conditional on treating others with respect.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive, including when reviewing code or disagreeing about technical decisions.
- Critique ideas and implementation details rather than attacking people.
- Explain technical feedback clearly and assume good faith unless there is a reason not to.
- Accept reasonable correction, acknowledge mistakes, and focus discussions on improving the project.
- Respect other people's time, privacy, and boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant to VantaOS and follow the rules of the platform being used.
- Give accurate credit for other people's work and follow applicable licenses.
- Report security vulnerabilities responsibly rather than publishing details that could put users at risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or deliberate personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language or conduct targeting personal characteristics.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or sharing sexual content with the community.
- Doxxing, publishing private information, or threatening to do so without explicit consent.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, bad-faith abuse of review processes, or repeated personal hostility.
- Impersonation, deliberate misrepresentation, or knowingly false reports intended to harm others.
- Encouraging violence or other targeted abuse.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

Strong technical disagreement, respectful criticism, and good-faith reports of bugs or misconduct are not violations simply because they are uncomfortable or unpopular.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to VantaOS repositories, issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, project communication channels, and other spaces where a person is representing or participating in the project. It also applies when conduct outside those spaces materially affects the safety or ability of community members to participate.

## Reporting a concern

Report misconduct to the project maintainer through a private communication channel available from the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal details or security vulnerability details in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, follow the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Please include enough context to understand what happened, such as relevant links, dates, and a description of the behavior. Share only information you are comfortable providing. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and discreetly as practical; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Responses will be proportionate to the nature and impact of the behavior and may include:

1. A private reminder or request to stop.
2. A formal warning.
3. Temporary limits on participation or interaction.
4. Removal of comments or other contributions.
5. Temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Serious misconduct may result in immediate action. Enforcement decisions should explain the relevant behavior and any applicable restriction when it is safe and practical to do so. Retaliation against people who report concerns in good faith is prohibited.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in VantaOS project spaces, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and cooperate with reasonable efforts to keep the community respectful and productive.
